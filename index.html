Statement & Exhibitions

ARTIST STATEMENT

My practice as an artist is deeply rooted in emotion, personal experience, and the connections that shape our lives. I view art as a reflection of the soul—a medium through which I can process and articulate experiences that transcend words. Much of my work serves as a tribute to the people, memories, and moments that leave a lasting mark on me, honoring both loved ones and the memory of those lost. Through intentional research, symbolic choices, and deliberate medium selection, I transform complex emotions into tangible creations. I approach every subject with love and respect above all else, aiming to create works that bridge a connection with viewers and allow them to find their own meaning within the story.
